# Product rules

1. **A true Pokémon battle clone first.** The game reproduces the battles of the generation it models,
   mechanics and quirks included, before extending them; Gen 1 is the current target. Extensions draw on
   roguelikes and roguelites (progression, randomness, replayability), autobattlers (positioning, automated
   phases, team synergy) and the Pokémon Infinite Fusion mod (fusion, generated sprites and stats, expanded
   movepools).
2. **Run state belongs to the run.** The bag, the wallet and the party reset when a run ends. Unlocks that
   carry across runs are the eventual direction but not planned; don't design them unprompted. No saves that
   survive a server restart during the current development phase.
