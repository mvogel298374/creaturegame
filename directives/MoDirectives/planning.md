# Planning rules

1. **All planned work lives in `docs/TODO.md` before it starts.** Every item has an entry that meets the
   Definition of Ready (`docs/DEFINITION_OF_READY.md`). Work discovered along the way gets its own entry
   first. Enforced by the Ready guardrail.
2. **Big plans are written out in full.** Once I approve a large plan, the whole plan goes into
   `docs/TODO.md`, not only into a session file, so the work can resume cold.
3. **Mark what I'll redesign.** Parts of a plan I've said I'll challenge are marked provisional and are not
   implemented until we've redesigned them.
