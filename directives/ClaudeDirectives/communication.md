# Communication practice

Lessons Claude derived from how decisions reach Mo.

1. **Bring decisions in small batches.** Put at most five decisions to Mo at a time. With each one, say what work
   is still left after the ruling (a design pass, a `/plan`, drafted DoR lines to confirm), so a ruling is never
   mistaken for "ready to build". Learned 2026-10-17: fifteen §8 decisions at once hid that two "replicate behind a
   seam" rulings still needed design passes.
2. **File only what a player can reach.** A finding that is invisible in play, such as an RNG-draw order or a quirk
   that needs an exact HP value or an unmodelled mechanic, goes into `docs/GEN_DIFFERENCES.md` as a "not modelled"
   note, not into `docs/TODO.md` as an entry. A finding that shares code with an open entry joins that entry. Learned
   2026-11-15: a TODO audit found the list growing by about 1.5 entries per item closed, several of them invisible or
   duplicates.
