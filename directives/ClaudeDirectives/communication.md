# Communication practice

Lessons Claude derived from how decisions reach Mo.

1. **Bring decisions in small batches.** Put at most five decisions to Mo at a time. With each one, say what work
   is still left after the ruling (a design pass, a `/plan`, drafted DoR lines to confirm), so a ruling is never
   mistaken for "ready to build". Learned 2026-10-17: fifteen §8 decisions at once hid that two "replicate behind a
   seam" rulings still needed design passes.
